Common questions

Is dawg free? Yes. Logging, PRs and progress are free and work fully offline. The Founding Dawg membership adds sync and backup across your devices.

How do I cancel my membership? On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → dawg. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I got a new phone. How do I get my workouts back? Members: sign in with the same email or Apple ID and everything syncs down. If you bought on this Apple ID but never signed in, use “Restore purchase” on the membership screen.

How do I delete my account? In dawg: Settings → Delete Account. It removes your account and synced data. See the privacy policy.

Can I export my data? Yes: Settings → Export Workouts saves a spreadsheet (CSV).
