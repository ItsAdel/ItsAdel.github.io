Common questions

Is dawg free?

Yes. Workout logging, PRs, progress, calorie tracking and monthly report cards are free and work offline. The Founding Dawg membership adds backup and sync across your devices, the community food database, streak freezes and every past report card. See what's new.

How do I cancel my membership?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → dawg. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I got a new phone. How do I get my workouts back?

Members: sign in with the same email or Apple ID and everything syncs down. If you bought on this Apple ID but never signed in, use “Restore purchase” on the membership screen.

How do I delete my account?

In dawg: Settings → Delete Account. It removes your account and synced data. See the privacy policy.

Can I export my data?

Yes: Settings → Export Workouts and Export Food Log each save a spreadsheet (CSV).

How do I change my calorie or protein goal?

Open the Food tab and tap your goal to recalculate it, or set the numbers yourself. Every two weeks the check-in card suggests an adjustment based on your weight trend.

A food is missing or a barcode won't scan.

Online food search needs a connection to look up USDA and Open Food Facts. If a food still isn't there, use Create Food or Quick Add, and feel free to email me the barcode.

A food's numbers look wrong.

For foods you've saved, tap the food and use Edit Food to fix your copy. If it's a community food, members can tap “Numbers look wrong?” to report it, and foods with enough reports are hidden automatically.

How do community foods work?

Founding Dawg members can search foods shared by other members, and share the foods they create (Settings → Food → Share Foods I Create). Every shared food is checked automatically and duplicates are merged. Who shared a food is never shown to anyone.

What are streak freezes?

Founding Dawg members earn one streak freeze a month and can save up to 3. If you miss a week, one is used automatically so your streak keeps going. Going on vacation? Tap your streak and use Plan a Break.

Where's my monthly report card?

When a month ends, your pup's report card for it shows up on the Workout tab, ready to share. Last month's card is free; Founding Dawg members keep every month's card to look back on.
