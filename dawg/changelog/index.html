dawg 1.2

Coming soon

Our biggest update yet. dawg now tracks your food as well as your lifts, and everything below is free unless it says Founding Dawg.

🥗 Food tracking, free for everyone

  • A new Food tab: calories left for the day, protein, carbs and fat, and your meals
  • Search foods from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, scan barcodes, quick add, or create your own
  • My Foods and favorites, and copy a whole meal in one tap
  • A daily goal from your body and training: a slight surplus to build muscle, a deficit to get lean
  • Workouts add the calories you burned to that day
  • Weigh-ins, your weight trend, and a two-week check-in that tunes your goal
  • Full days: log all four meals and your pup celebrates
  • Progress → Nutrition: a calendar of meals logged, your averages and your weight trend
  • Export your food log to CSV

📋 A plan from day one

  • Onboarding asks what you're training for, how often and where, then builds your plan (3 days a week? Push, Pull, Legs)
  • Your plan shows your workouts and your daily calories and protein together
  • A template library of ready-made programs, adjusted for your goal, equipment and level

🔥 Streaks and report cards

  • Tap your streak to see this week, the last 16 weeks, your best and your next milestone
  • Monthly report cards from your pup: your workouts, top PR, goals hit, food and weight for the month, ready to share as an image

🎨 A fresh look

  • A bolder design with new colors and type, and your pup as a sticker on the Workout tab
  • A new tab bar: History, Food, Workout, Progress and Settings, with Workout front and center

☁️ Backups you can see

  • Settings shows when you last backed up
  • A heads-up whenever your workouts aren't backing up, with how many are only on this phone and how to fix it
  • Signing out with changes that haven't uploaded yet warns you first and offers Back Up Now

🐾 New for Founding Dawg members

  • The community food database: foods shared and checked by members. Share your own (who shared it is never shown) and report numbers that look wrong
  • Streak freezes: earn one a month and save up to 3. A missed week uses one automatically, or plan a break before a vacation
  • Every report card to look back on, not just last month's
  • All on top of backup and sync across your devices

🔧 Also

  • Fixes and small improvements can now reach you without waiting for an App Store update
  • Deleting your account asks you to type "delete dawg" first, so it can't happen by accident

dawg 1.0

November 10, 2026

dawg's first release: a simple, friendly workout log with a puppy who's always happy to see you.

  • Log sets in seconds with last time's weights and reps beside you
  • Templates, and swapping or reordering exercises mid-workout
  • Smart exercise search that understands "ohp", "tris" and typos
  • A rest timer that starts itself, on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Personal records, lift charts, sets per muscle and a muscle map
  • Weekly goals and week streaks
  • Plate calculator, pounds or kilograms, and CSV export
  • Works fully offline
  • Founding Dawg membership: backup and sync, with Sign in with Apple or an email code
