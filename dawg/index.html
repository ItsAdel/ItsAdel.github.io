dawg 🐾 Coming soon to the App Store

Your gym buddy, with a wagging tail.

dawg is a simple, friendly workout logger. Log every set, watch your lifts climb, and let your puppy cheer on every personal record.

  • Fast logging: sets, reps, weights, rest timer on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Progress you can feel: personal records, lift trends and a muscle map of your week.
  • Free and private: use it fully offline, nothing leaves your phone.
  • Founding Dawg membership: sync and back up across devices, for every app in the family.
