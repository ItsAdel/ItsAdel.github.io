How it works

Built for the gym floor, not the couch.

1

Log sets in seconds

Tap through your sets with last time's weights and reps right beside you. Start from a template or build the workout as you go.

  • Swap or reorder exercises mid-workout
  • Find anything fast, even “ohp” or “tris”
  • Create your own exercises
2

A rest timer that runs itself

Finish a set and the countdown starts on its own. Glance at your Lock Screen or Dynamic Island and you know exactly when to go again.

  • Live on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Add or skip time with one tap
3

Watch your lifts climb

Charts for every lift, so you can see the progress you can feel. Every personal record gets celebrated the moment you hit it.

  • Estimated 1RM, heaviest weight and best volume
  • Weekly goals and streaks
4

Know what you've trained

A muscle map of your week shows what's worked and what's been skipped, so leg day can't hide from you.

  • Sets per muscle, week by week
  • Your full history, one tap away
5

Your pup is always proud

Finish a workout and get a recap of everything you did, plus one very happy puppy. Paw prints fill up as you hit your weekly goal.

  • Duration, volume and sets at a glance
  • Confetti when you earn it
