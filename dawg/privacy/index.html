dawg Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026. Published at https://itsadel.com/dawg/privacy/.

dawg is a workout logger and calorie tracker made by Adel Akbar, an independent developer in British Columbia, Canada ("I", "me"). This policy explains what dawg collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: dawg doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and never sells your data. In offline mode, your workouts and food diary never leave your phone. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, only that search or barcode goes to the food databases, never your diary or who you are.

1. Two ways to use dawg

Offline (free). Your workouts, exercises, templates, food diary, weigh-ins, goals and settings are stored only on your phone. dawg doesn't send them anywhere. If you open the membership offer, the app contacts our purchase provider (RevenueCat, see section 5) to show the current price; nothing about your workouts or food is included.

With an account (Founding Dawg membership). Your data is stored on your phone and synced to our server so it's backed up and available on your other devices.

2. Food search and barcodes (both ways)

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, dawg sends only the text you typed or the barcode number directly from your phone to two public food databases, USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, to find the nutrition facts. Like any website, they also see your phone's IP address. They don't receive your account, your diary, or anything that identifies you to us. Foods you create, and foods you've logged before, work offline.

The camera is used only to read barcodes. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.

3. What we collect with an account

  • Sign-in details: your email address, or, if you use Sign in with Apple, an identifier from Apple and the email Apple shares (which may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email").
  • Your training data: workouts, sets (weights, reps, times, distances, notes), exercises you create, and templates.
  • Your food diary: what you ate, how much, which meal and when, with its nutrition; foods you create and favorites.
  • Health and body details: weigh-ins, and, if you enter them, your sex (used only for the calorie formula), birth year, height, activity level, and calorie and macro goals.
  • Settings, such as units, rest timer preferences, reminders and your pup's name.
  • Membership status: whether your membership is active and when it renews or ends.
  • Technical data: our server keeps standard logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) for security and troubleshooting.

Food, weight and body details are health information. We treat them as sensitive: they're used only to run dawg for you, never for advertising, and never shared except with the services in section 5 that store them for us.

We don't collect your contacts, location, photos, Apple Health data, or advertising identifiers. dawg has no analytics or advertising code.

4. How we use it

Only to run dawg: to sign you in, sync and back up your data, work out your calorie goal and check-ins, unlock membership features, keep the service secure, and answer you if you contact support. No ads, no profiling, no selling, no sharing for anyone else's marketing. The goal, check-in and workout-calorie math runs on your phone.

Reminders, such as the rest timer and the "miss you" nudge, are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Account data is kept while you have an account.
  • Delete your account any time in dawg: Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your account and synced data, workouts and food alike, from our database immediately and revokes Sign in with Apple. Backups containing it are overwritten within 7 days.
  • Offline data stays on your phone until you delete it or the app.
  • You can export your data at any time as spreadsheet files: Settings → Export Workouts and Export Food Log.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how it's handled, by emailing privacy@itsadel.com. Depending on where you live (for example Canada, under PIPEDA, or the EU/UK, under the GDPR), you may have additional rights, including complaining to your privacy regulator.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database only lets each account read and change its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects it, as the law requires.

9. Children

dawg isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Not medical advice

dawg's calorie goals, check-ins and workout calorie estimates are for general wellness, not medical advice. Talk to a health professional before big changes to your diet or training.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
