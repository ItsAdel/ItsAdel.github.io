munch Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. Published at https://itsadel.com/munch/privacy/.

munch is a calorie counter made by Adel Akbar, an independent developer in British Columbia, Canada ("I", "me"). This policy explains what munch collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: munch doesn’t show ads, doesn’t track you across apps or websites, and never sells your data. Without an account, your food diary never leaves your phone. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, only that search or barcode goes to the food databases, never your diary or who you are.

1. Two ways to use munch

Offline (free). Everything you enter is stored only on your phone: the foods you log, your own foods and favorites, weigh-ins, exercise, water, your goals and body details, and settings. munch doesn’t send any of it anywhere. If you open the membership offer, the app contacts our purchase provider (RevenueCat, see section 5) to show the current price; nothing from your diary is included.

With an account (Founding Member). Your data is stored on your phone and synced to our server, so it’s backed up and available on your other devices.

2. Food search and barcodes (both ways)

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, munch sends only the text you typed or the barcode number directly from your phone to two public food databases, USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, to find the nutrition facts. Like any website, they also see your phone’s IP address. They don’t receive your account, your diary, or anything that identifies you to us. You can turn this off in munch: You → Online Food Search. Your own foods still work offline.

The camera is used only to read barcodes. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.

3. What we collect with an account

  • Sign-in details: your email address, or, if you use Sign in with Apple, an identifier from Apple and the email Apple shares (which may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email").
  • Your food diary: what you ate, how much, which meal and when, with its nutrition; foods you create and favorites.
  • Health and body details: weigh-ins, and, if you enter them, your sex (used only for the calorie formula), birth year, height, activity level, goal weight, and calorie and macro goals.
  • Exercise and water you log.
  • Workouts from dawg (members who also use dawg): if you use our workout app dawg with the same account, munch reads your finished dawg workouts from the past week (name, start and finish times, and the number of completed sets) to estimate calories burned. It doesn’t read weights, reps or notes. Turn it off in munch: You → Workouts from dawg.
  • Settings, such as units, reminders and your hamster’s name.
  • Membership status: whether your membership is active and when it renews or ends.
  • Technical data: our server keeps standard logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) for security and troubleshooting.

Food, weight and body details are health information. We treat them as sensitive: they’re used only to run munch for you, never for advertising, and never shared except with the services in section 5 that store them for us.

We don’t collect your contacts, location, photos, Apple Health data, or advertising identifiers. munch has no analytics or advertising code.

4. How we use it

Only to run munch: to sign you in, sync and back up your data, work out your calorie goal and check-ins, unlock membership features, keep the service secure, and answer you if you contact support. No ads, no profiling, no selling, no sharing for anyone else’s marketing. The goal and check-in math runs on your phone.

The "miss you" reminder, if you turn it on, is scheduled on your phone. No server sends it.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Account data is kept while you have an account.
  • Delete your account any time in munch: You → Delete Account. This deletes your account and synced data from our database immediately and revokes Sign in with Apple. Backups containing it are overwritten within 7 days. Your account is shared with dawg, so deleting it removes your dawg account and data too.
  • Offline data stays on your phone until you delete it or the app.
  • Want a copy of your data? Email us and we’ll send it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how it’s handled, by emailing privacy@itsadel.com. Depending on where you live (for example Canada, under PIPEDA, or the EU/UK, under the GDPR), you may have additional rights, including complaining to your privacy regulator.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database only lets each account read and change its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects it, as the law requires.

9. Children

munch is for ages 13 and up. It isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Not medical advice

munch’s calorie goals and check-ins are estimates for general wellness, not medical advice. Talk to a health professional before big changes to your diet.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app.
