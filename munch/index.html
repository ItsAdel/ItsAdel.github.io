How it works

The basics, done really well.

Everything you need to eat on target, nothing you have to wade through.

📷

Scan it

Point at a barcode and the label’s nutrition is there, from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Strongest on US and Canadian groceries.

🔎

Or just type it

“What did you munch?” Search your foods and the databases at once, re-log yesterday’s breakfast in a tap, or drop in a number.

🎯

A goal that fits you

A minute of questions sets your daily goal. Every two weeks, munch checks your weight trend and suggests a better number if it’s off.

🍽️

Meals at a glance

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, each with its total. Protein, carbs and fat shown as what’s left, not more charts.

🐹

A hamster who cares

Name your buddy. They munch along when you log, cheer your streaks, and send one gentle nudge if you’ve been away.

📴

Works offline

Your diary lives on your phone. No account needed, and it stays there unless you choose to sync.
