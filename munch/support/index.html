Common questions

Is munch free?

Yes. Logging, barcode scanning, your goal and progress are free and work offline. The Founding Member membership adds backup and sync across your devices, and brings in workouts from dawg.

How is my daily calorie goal worked out?

From the details you share in setup: the Mifflin-St Jeor formula for the calories your body uses at rest, times your day-to-day activity, minus (or plus) enough for your chosen pace. Workouts you log are added on top. Every two weeks munch compares your weight trend with the plan and suggests a new goal if they’ve drifted apart. Change it any time in You → Daily Calories.

A barcode wasn’t found, or a food’s numbers look wrong.

munch looks barcodes up in USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, which cover most US and Canadian groceries but not every store brand or new product. If it’s missing, tap Create Food: the barcode is filled in, and munch finds your version next time you scan it. If the numbers look off, open the food and edit it; your copy is what munch uses from then on.

I’m a Founding Dawg. Do I need to pay again?

No. Membership covers every app in the family. Sign in to munch with the same email or Apple ID you use in dawg and you’re already a member.

How do workouts from dawg work?

For members who use both apps with the same account. When you open munch, it brings in your finished dawg workouts from the past week as exercise, with calories estimated from how long and how busy each workout was. Tap the ⓘ on one to see the math. Swipe one away to leave it out. Turn it off in You → Workouts from dawg.

How do I cancel my membership?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → munch. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Your diary stays on your phone either way.

I got a new phone. How do I get my diary back?

Members: sign in with the same email or Apple ID and everything syncs down. If you bought on this Apple ID but never signed in, use “Restore purchase” on the membership screen. Without an account, your diary lives only on the old phone.

How do I delete my account?

In munch: You → Delete Account. It removes your account and synced data. The account is shared with dawg, so your dawg data is deleted too. See the privacy policy.
